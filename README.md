# EDS223 Homework Assignment 3: Prioritizing Potential Aquaculture
This repository houses the work completed by Rachel Miller for the EDS 223 homework assignment 3. The aim is to analyze sea surface temperature and depth data wihtin Exclusive Economic Zones (EEZ) on the West Coast of the United States of America to identify areas that are most suitable for aquaculture of defined species.

## Data Access
The data was sourced from a Google Drive [link](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1u-iwnPDbe6ZK7wSFVMI-PpCKaRQ3RVmg/view), but originates from:

1. NOAA’s 5km Daily Global Satellite Sea Surface Temperature Anomaly v3.1link: [link](https://coralreefwatch.noaa.gov/product/5km/index_5km_ssta.php)
2. The General Bathymetric Chart of the Oceans (GEBCO). [link](https://www.gebco.net/data-products/gridded-bathymetry-data#area)
3. Exclusive Economic Zones off of the west coast of US from Marineregions.org [link](https://www.marineregions.org/eez.php)
